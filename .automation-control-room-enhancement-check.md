## Automation Control Room enhancement verification

The final production build passed after adding interactive node details, neon glow layers, and mobile responsive composition.

Each of the 13 node cards now has a keyboard-focusable target, an accessible label, a tooltip description, and a status message. The tooltip appears on hover or `:focus-visible`, with a mobile-specific downward placement to avoid clipping.

Signal paths now use a brighter restrained accent stroke with glow filters, moving particles have a stronger neon halo, and the AI decision core has layered accent lighting while retaining the charcoal surface treatment.

Desktop verification confirms the enhanced control room remains balanced in the hero. Mobile CSS switches to a dedicated 780px control-room composition: inputs span the upper region, the AI core sits centered below them, actions and outputs form two lower columns, and a separate mobile SVG uses vertically aligned endpoints for those zones. Reduced-motion rules disable the animated effects when requested by the user’s system.
