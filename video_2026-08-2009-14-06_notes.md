# Recording diagnosis notes

Source: `/home/ubuntu/upload/2026-08-2009-14-06.mp4`

The recording is 1920x1080 at 60 fps and lasts about 4.18 seconds. The contact sheet shows the hero diagram captured repeatedly during the first several seconds, followed by an OBS window appearing in the final sample frame.

Observed visual issues in the diagram:

1. The four stages are visible, but the visual flow feels compressed around the AI core and the right-side cards. Input connectors converge into a tight cluster at the core, and core-to-workflow connectors leave from nearby points, producing a pinched fan rather than a calm, evenly distributed flow.
2. The workflow/output separation is visually insufficient in the recording. The two right-side card columns read as a crowded block, so the connector channel is not clearly legible at normal browser scale.
3. The output header appears slightly offset relative to its card column in the recording.
4. The animated connector treatment appears to jump or snap visually on curved paths. The likely reason is the traveling dashed stroke/offset pattern moving over paths with very different lengths and curvature; the dash phase changes at different speeds and can look like a discontinuity at bends. This is not evidence of a React lifecycle freeze.
5. Card secondary text is very small at the recording’s browser scale, especially on the right-side cards. The visual hierarchy is therefore dominated by card blocks rather than readable labels/details.
6. The core has ambient motion, but the AI Decision Core label and descriptive text remain static. This creates a perceived mismatch where the connectors/core halo are active but the center’s semantic state does not visibly respond.
7. The core looks slightly low relative to the full five-card input stack in the recording. This is a composition/baseline issue rather than a connector endpoint calculation issue.

Implementation comparison:

- Current source uses a static SVG viewBox `0 0 1000 500`.
- Input cards are positioned at x=24 with width 250 and y=100,178,256,334,412; their right edge is x=274.
- AI core is translated to `(318,108)` with local center `(100,142)`, giving absolute center `(418,250)` and outer orbit radius `91`.
- Workflow cards are at x=580 with width 160, so their right edge is x=740.
- Output cards are at x=790 with width 170, giving a deliberate 50px SVG-space gap.
- There are 13 connector paths. Their core-facing endpoints are calculated against the orbit perimeter; the current endpoint geometry is not the main remaining defect.
- Connector animation is CSS `stroke-dashoffset` with `stroke-dasharray: 8 120`, linear duration 3.4s, and staggered delays. This is the most likely cause of the perceived snapping/jumping.
- Responsive behavior currently scales the complete SVG proportionally below 860px rather than stacking stages vertically. It avoids overflow, but makes card text very small on mobile.

Likely diagnosis: the recording’s main problem is not a startup render freeze. It is a composition-density and motion-treatment problem: the four-stage diagram is structurally present, but the core-to-card fan and the dashed-stroke animation make the flow look cramped and unstable at the rendered scale. The safest next fix would be to improve the spacing/visual channel and replace or soften the dash travel with a smoother, path-consistent signal treatment, without changing the core or card content.
